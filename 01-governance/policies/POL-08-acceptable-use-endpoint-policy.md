## Purpose

To set the rules for using ShopNova laptops and IT services safely, in the office and when working remotely.

## Scope

All staff laptops (A-04, about 40 devices) and every person who uses them or ShopNova IT services.

## Roles

The **Head of IT** owns this policy and manages the laptops. **IT administrators** configure and support
them. **All staff** follow the rules below and report problems.

## Policy statements

1. **Managed devices only.** Customer data is accessed only from ShopNova-managed laptops with:
   - full-disk encryption (BitLocker);
   - endpoint protection (EDR);
   - automatic updates under the POL-02 deadlines;
   - a screen lock after 5 minutes.
2. **No local admin rights.** Users have no administrator rights on their laptop. Software is installed by IT.
3. **Customer data stays in the systems.** Customer data is not copied to local disks, USB sticks or
   personal cloud storage, except where IT has approved it for a specific task.
4. **Remote work.** Remote work uses the company VPN, never public or shared computers. Screens must not
   be visible to others.
5. **Phishing and incidents.** Suspicious emails are reported with the "Report phishing" button. Lost or
   stolen laptops are reported to IT within one hour (POL-05).
6. **Personal use.** Personal use is allowed within reason, if it does not harm security. Staff are told
   which logs are collected for security monitoring.

## Compliance

KPI-07 tracks training completion. IT checks device compliance (encryption, updates) every month through device management.

## Review

The Head of IT reviews this policy every year, and after any endpoint-related incident.
