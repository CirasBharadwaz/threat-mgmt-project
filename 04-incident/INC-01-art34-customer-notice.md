# INC-01 notice to customers (content of Art. 34(2) GDPR, sent voluntarily)

**Channel:** email to all registered customers, plus a notice on the shop's start page.
**Status:** simulated sending (lab exercise).
**Legal basis for sending:** Art. 34 does not require it, because the breach assessment rates the risk as
not high (`INC-01-breach-assessment.md`, section 4). The Managing Director decided to inform customers
anyway, because they can only protect themselves against phishing if they know. The text covers
everything Art. 34(2) requires: what happened, the DPO contact, the likely consequences, and the measures
taken. It is in clear and plain language.

---

**Subject: Important information about your ShopNova customer account**

Dear customer,

we are writing to tell you about a security incident that affects your customer account.

**What happened?**
On 17 November 2026, an unauthorised person used the stolen password of one of our support staff accounts
to access our shop system for a few seconds. In that time the person downloaded a list of customer
accounts. We noticed it within seconds and closed the access within a minute.

**Which of your data was affected?**
Your name or username, your email address, the type of your account, and the internet (IP) address of
your last login.

**Your password and your payment details were NOT affected.** We never show passwords in our system, and
we do not store card details; your payment is handled by our payment provider.

**What could this mean for you?**
You may receive fake emails that pretend to come from ShopNova, for example about an order, a refund or a
payment problem. Their aim is usually to get your password or card details.

**What should you do?**
- Be careful with emails, text messages or calls that claim to be from ShopNova and ask you to click a link, log in or pay.
- We will **never** ask for your password or card details by email.
- If in doubt, do not click. Open our website by typing the address yourself and log in there.
- You do not need to change your password because of this incident. If you use the same password on other websites, it is always a good idea to use different passwords.

**What are we doing?**
We closed the access at once and secured the affected account. We have told the Berlin data protection
authority. We are now removing extended rights from support accounts and adding a second login factor
for all staff accounts, so that a stolen password alone is no longer enough.

**Questions?**
Our data protection officer will answer your questions: [name], [email], [phone].
You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

We are sorry for this incident and for any worry it may cause.

Your ShopNova team
